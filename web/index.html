About this tool

Search Manitoba properties (excluding Winnipeg, which has its own portal) by civic address, municipality, roll #, legal description, or certificate of title. Each result is enriched with its top-2 zoning districts (with coverage %) and its primary development-plan designation, plus the bylaw numbers behind each. Legal description and certificate-of-title search are backed by a generated MAO scrape index; substring matches resolve to live parcel records via Roll Entry, so geometry and assessment fields are always current.

Roll Entry is queried live for parcel geometry and current assessment fields on every search. The live parcel source is Roll Entry; enrichment comes from Zoning By-Laws, and Development Plan Designations. Municipality + category dropdowns and the overlay layers (zoning, dev-plan, contaminated sites, traffic flow, full muni parcel fabric) are cached in your browser for up to 7 days so repeat work in the same area stays snappy. The Clear button wipes that cache.

Disclaimer

This site is provided without warranty and is for general information only. Data is queried live from the Province of Manitoba's open data portal and may be incomplete, out of date, or inaccurate. Zoning and development-plan designations are matched to each parcel by spatial overlap; coverage percentages are computed client-side and assume both layers are spatially aligned to the parcel polygons (in practice they are usually but not always, since bylaw amendments occasionally land in the source data weeks behind the actual zoning change). Do not rely on these results without independent verification from Manitoba Assessment Online, the municipality's planning office, or a qualified professional.

Street number. On its own it's a "contains" match, so 1106 finds "1 106 E ROAD 71 N" however the number is spaced. Fill To as well for an exact number (same value) or a range (different). Street number upper bound (inclusive). Tab here after From and it auto-fills with the same value (highlighted) for an exact-number search; overwrite for a range. Clear it and From becomes a "contains" match instead. Substring match against the street name portion of Property_Address (e.g. 'MAIN ST', 'DRURY', 'ROAD 96W').
Roll # — exact match. Two punctuation rules, and they mean opposite things. SEPARATE different properties with a comma, space or newline — so a column pasted straight from a spreadsheet lands as one chip per roll (e.g. 83100, 85200, 93000 = three properties). JOIN rolls of ONE property with + & or | — e.g. 83100+83200 or 83100&83200 — and they stay a single chip, shade together on the map, share one badge number, and produce ONE combined Parcel Snapshot instead of one image per roll. Press Enter, comma or space to commit a chip; Backspace on an empty input removes the last one. Accepts both 3600 and 3600.000. For a cross-muni list, use Import List… (under Advanced searches) to paste 2–4 columns and have each row's muni resolved automatically.

Filter to one or more zone codes — the same values the Zoning and Zoning 2 columns show. The list holds only the codes your current results actually contain, so it changes with every upload. Additional filters
Filter uploaded sales to rows whose Property Address contains this string. Case-insensitive substring match.
Sale Price: Minimum total sale price (the Consideration from the uploaded CSV). For a multi-parcel sale this is the whole transaction, so the entire sale passes or fails together. Empty = no lower limit. Maximum total sale price (the Consideration from the uploaded CSV). Sales with no readable price drop out while either bound is set. Empty = no upper limit.
Bldg Threshold: Vacant land proxy threshold, applied by the Vacant Only checkbox above. In % mode (default), a parcel reads as vacant when Buildings ÷ Total < this percent. In $ mode, when Buildings $ is below this dollar value. Heuristic only — verify with the municipality.
Exclude sales whose Sale ÷ Assessed Total ratio exceeds the chosen cap. Useful when buildings sold before the assessment caught up (Sale/Asmt >> 1). Leave on "Max Sale/Asmt" for no cap. Applies alongside Vacant Only.
km Far-Flung marks multi-parcel sales whose parcels lie farther apart than the km threshold, measured between parcel centroids. Portfolio and estate sales sweep up land across a wide area, so their blended $/Acre is not a local comparable. Blank or 0 turns the marking off, and marking alone removes nothing — tick Exclude to drop the flagged sales from the table, the map and the CSV export. Sales whose spread can't be measured (a parcel with no geometry) are never excluded.

    Paste columns from a spreadsheet, or upload a CSV/TSV file. Roll # is required, plus at least one of Municipality (name), Muni #, Title #, or Legal Description. Supplying a Muni # (or a recognised Municipality name) skips the legal-index lookup and is the most reliable input. Multi-parcel sales — one row with several stacked rolls — import as a single shaded group.
