About this tool

Search Manitoba properties (excluding Winnipeg, which has its own portal) by civic address, municipality, roll #, legal description, or certificate of title. Each result is enriched with its top-2 zoning districts (with coverage %) and its primary development-plan designation, plus the bylaw numbers behind each. Legal description and certificate-of-title search are backed by a generated MAO scrape index; substring matches resolve to live parcel records via Roll Entry, so geometry and assessment fields are always current.

Roll Entry is queried live for parcel geometry and current assessment fields on every search. The live parcel source is Roll Entry; enrichment comes from Zoning By-Laws, and Development Plan Designations. Municipality + category dropdowns and the overlay layers (zoning, dev-plan, contaminated sites, traffic flow, full muni parcel fabric) are cached in your browser for up to 7 days so repeat work in the same area stays snappy. The Clear button wipes that cache.

Disclaimer

This site is provided without warranty and is for general information only. Data is queried live from the Province of Manitoba's open data portal and may be incomplete, out of date, or inaccurate. Zoning and development-plan designations are matched to each parcel by spatial overlap; coverage percentages are computed client-side and assume both layers are spatially aligned to the parcel polygons (in practice they are usually but not always, since bylaw amendments occasionally land in the source data weeks behind the actual zoning change). Do not rely on these results without independent verification from Manitoba Assessment Online, the municipality's planning office, or a qualified professional.

Roll # — exact match. Two punctuation rules, and they mean opposite things. SEPARATE different properties with a comma, space or newline — so a column pasted straight from a spreadsheet lands as one chip per roll (e.g. 83100, 85200, 93000 = three properties). JOIN rolls of ONE property with + & or | — e.g. 83100+83200 or 83100&83200 — and they stay a single chip, shade together on the map, share one badge number, and produce ONE combined Parcel Snapshot instead of one image per roll. Press Enter, comma or space to commit a chip; Backspace on an empty input removes the last one. Accepts both 3600 and 3600.000. For a cross-muni list, use Import List… (under Advanced searches) to paste 2–4 columns and have each row's muni resolved automatically.
