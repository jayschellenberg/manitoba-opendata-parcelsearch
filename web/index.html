    About this tool

    Search Manitoba properties (excluding Winnipeg, which has its own portal) by civic address, municipality, roll #, legal description, or certificate of title. Each result is enriched with its top-2 zoning districts (with coverage %) and its primary development-plan designation, plus the bylaw numbers behind each. Legal-description and certificate-of-title search are backed by a generated MAO scrape index — substring matches there resolve to live parcel records via Roll Entry, so geometry and assessment fields are always current.

    Roll Entry is queried live for parcel geometry and current assessment fields on every search. The live parcel source is Roll Entry; enrichment comes from Zoning By-Laws, and Development Plan Designations. Municipality + category dropdowns and the overlay layers (zoning, dev-plan, contaminated sites, traffic flow, full muni parcel fabric) are cached in your browser for up to 7 days so repeat work in the same area stays snappy. The Clear button wipes that cache.

    Disclaimer: This site is provided without warranty and is for general information only. Data is queried live from the Province of Manitoba's open data portal and may be incomplete, out of date, or inaccurate. Zoning and development-plan designations are matched to each parcel by spatial overlap; coverage percentages are computed client-side and assume both layers are spatially aligned to the parcel polygons (in practice they are usually but not always — bylaw amendments occasionally land in the source data weeks behind the actual zoning change). Do not rely on these results without independent verification from Manitoba Assessment Online, the municipality's planning office, or a qualified professional.
